TITLE: Dialog Global Roaming Data Passport

Overview:
Dialog Global Data Passport delivers predictable, capped daily and weekly international data rates for Sri Lankan travelers going abroad. It replaces unpredictable pay-as-you-go data roaming fees with fixed daily buckets that activate automatically upon connection with foreign partner carriers.

Roaming Rates & Tiers:
- Daily Roaming Passport: USD 5.00 per day (+ local telecom taxes) for 2 GB high-speed daily data.
- Weekly Roaming Passport: USD 25.00 for 7 days, including 15 GB high-speed roaming data.
- Pay-As-You-Go Standard Rate (without Passport): USD 0.15 per MB.
- Outgoing Roaming Voice: USD 0.60 per minute to Sri Lanka; USD 0.40 per minute for local in-country calls.
- Incoming Roaming Voice: USD 0.30 per minute across all partner networks.

Activation Steps:
1. Ensure international roaming is activated on your SIM profile prior to departing Sri Lanka.
2. Dial #107# from your phone while in Sri Lanka or overseas.
3. Select option 1 'Roaming Services' -> Select 'Data Passports'.
4. Choose either '1-Day Unlimited Passport' or '7-Day Business Passport'.
5. Once your flight lands abroad, turn on 'Data Roaming' in your smartphone's Cellular/Mobile Data settings.

Best for: Business executives, corporate travelers, and vacationers traveling overseas who require reliable mobile maps, email, and messaging without bill shock.
