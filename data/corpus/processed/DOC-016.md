TITLE: Troubleshooting Guide: Unable to Place or Receive Voice Calls

Overview:
When outbound voice calls disconnect immediately with tones such as 'Call Ended', fast busy beeps, or incoming calls route straight to voicemail without ringing, follow this sequence of diagnostic steps.

Step-by-Step Resolution:

1. Check Outgoing Call Barring Settings:
 Ensure call barring has not been activated on your line. Dial the standard GSM cancellation code: #33*0000# and press Call. This deactivates all outgoing barring restrictions using the default factory PIN (0000).

2. Verify Call Forwarding Status:
 If inbound callers report reaching voicemail or a dead line, disable forwarding diversions:
 - Dial ##002# and press Send. This cancels all conditional and unconditional call diverts.

3. Enable or Disable VoLTE (Voice over LTE):
 Voice calls in modern networks require VoLTE. Navigate to Settings -> Mobile Networks -> VoLTE Calls -> Toggle ON. If VoLTE is already on and calls drop, toggle it OFF temporarily to route calls through standard 3G/2G circuit-switched fallback.

4. Inspect Credit Balance and Outstanding Dues:
 Prepaid customers: Dial #456# to verify account balance.
 Postpaid customers: Dial #145# to check credit limit status. If your credit limit has been exceeded, outgoing calls are suspended until an interim payment is settled.

5. Check 'Do Not Disturb' (DND) and Caller ID Settings:
 Verify that 'Do Not Disturb' or Focus mode is turned off. Under Phone App Settings -> Additional Settings -> Caller ID -> ensure it is set to 'Network Default' or 'Show Number', as hiding your caller ID can cause carrier firewalls to reject outbound calls.
