DOCUMENT ID: DOC-010
TITLE: Dialog Network Advisory: Central Province 4G/5G Infrastructure Upgrade

Document Reference: ADV-2026-CP-04
Date of Issue: October 2026
Affected Regions: Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Hatton, Bandarawela, and Matale Districts

Incident Summary:
Dialog Broadband Networks is undertaking planned transmission modernization and antenna array upgrades across high-altitude tower sites throughout the Central Province. This capital infrastructure project aims to expand 5G coverage corridors and strengthen rural 4G LTE capacity against adverse weather disruptions.

Impact Description:
Subscribers traveling or residing within the aforementioned districts may experience intermittent voice call drops, temporary fallback to 3G connectivity, and fluctuating mobile broadband data throughput between 01:00 AM and 05:30 AM on scheduled maintenance nights.

Timeline & Restoration:
- Phase 1 (Kandy Metro & Peradeniya): October 10 - October 15, 2026
- Phase 2 (Nuwara Eliya & Hatton Tea Valleys): October 16 - October 22, 2026
- Phase 3 (Bandarawela & Matale North): October 23 - October 28, 2026

Customer Action Recommended:
Subscribers relying on overnight voice communications are advised to enable Wi-Fi Calling (VoWiFi) on their devices. Emergency services (119 and 110) remain accessible throughout maintenance windows via neighbor-cell fallbacks. We regret any inconvenience caused during our work to improve regional connectivity.
