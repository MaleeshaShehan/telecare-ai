Overview:
Dialog maintains bilateral roaming agreements with tier-1 telecommunication operators spanning more than 160 countries globally. To guarantee high-speed 4G and 5G service abroad, your handset must register with our designated preferred roaming partners.

Major Partner Networks by Destination:
- United Kingdom: EE, Vodafone UK, O2 UK (4G/5G)
- United States: AT&T, T-Mobile USA (VoLTE mandatory; ensure VoLTE is enabled in device settings)
- United Arab Emirates: Etisalat, du (4G/5G)
- India: Bharti Airtel, Reliance Jio (4G/5G)
- Singapore: Singtel, StarHub (4G/5G)
- Australia: Telstra, Optus (4G/5G)
- Malaysia: Celcom, Maxis (4G)
- Japan: NTT Docomo, SoftBank (4G/5G)

Manual Network Selection Instructions:
If your mobile device fails to latch onto an authorized carrier automatically:
1. Open phone Settings -> Cellular / Mobile Data -> Network Selection.
2. Disable the 'Automatic' slider.
3. Allow the device up to two minutes to scan for all available local signals.
4. Select the designated partner listed above (e.g., choose 'Singtel' while in Singapore).
5. Once connected, toggle Airplane mode on and off to initialize data routing.
