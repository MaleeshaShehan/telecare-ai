Document Reference: ADV-2026-WP-11
Date of Issue: October 2026
Affected Regions: Colombo 01 (Fort), Colombo 02 (Slave Island), Colombo 03 (Kollupitiya)

Incident Summary:
In alignment with the Colombo Smart City Initiative, Dialog is deploying ultra-dense small cell nodes and underground dark-fiber backhaul across the Colombo financial district. This deployment delivers gigabit-speed wireless connectivity to commercial office towers and high-density transport hubs.

Impact Description:
Between 11:30 PM and 04:30 AM, specific roadside micro-cells will be isolated for fiber splicing. Users situated in ground-level outdoor areas along Galle Road and R.A. De Mel Mawatha may experience localized signal attenuation and brief 30-second handover delays during tower cutovers.

Duration & Expected Outcomes:
- Work Window: Monday through Thursday nights across a three-week rollout.
- Projected Completion: End of current calendar month.
- Target Benefits: 400% increase in downtown 5G microcell capacity, elimination of peak-hour data congestion along the coastal rail corridor, and improved VoLTE indoor call clarity.

No action is required from subscribers. Voice and emergency calling remain fully functional on secondary macro towers throughout the project period.
