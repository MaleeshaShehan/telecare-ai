Document Reference: ADV-2026-INT-09
Date of Issue: October 2026
Affected Services: International Gateway, Overseas Cloud Hosting, Low-Latency Gaming

Incident Summary:
The consortium managing the international undersea fiber-optic cable landing in Mt. Lavinia, Sri Lanka, has announced scheduled maritime maintenance to repair a fiber amplifier unit situated in the Indian Ocean.

Impact Description:
International bandwidth capacity will be temporarily constrained. While domestic internet browsing (such as Sri Lankan banking portals, local enterprise servers, and .lk domain infrastructure) remains entirely unaffected, customers may observe:
- Higher latency (ping times > 180ms) to European and North American cloud gaming servers.
- Slower download speeds when fetching files hosted on non-cached overseas servers.
- Minor video buffering on high-resolution 4K video streams from international content providers.

Mitigation Actions Taken by Dialog:
Dialog Network Operations Center (NOC) has successfully rerouted critical traffic through alternate trans-Pacific and Bay of Bengal fiber links. Critical banking, enterprise VPNs, and emergency health networks have been allocated guaranteed reserved bandwidth. Normal latency levels will be restored following completion of maritime splicing operations.
