TITLE: Dialog International Roaming Activation Guide

Overview:
Customers traveling outside Sri Lanka must enable the International Roaming (IR) facility on their mobile line prior to departing. Dialog offers convenient self-service provisioning through mobile channels to ensure uninterrupted phone service upon landing abroad.

Pre-requisites for Activation:
- Postpaid Accounts: Requires a refundable security deposit of LKR 15,000, or a valid active auto-debit credit card registered on file, or corporate clearance.
- Prepaid Accounts: No deposit needed; must maintain an active main prepaid balance exceeding LKR 1,000.

Step-by-Step Activation Instructions:
1. Self-Service via MyDialog App:
   a. Open the MyDialog app and tap the 'Menu' icon.
   b. Select 'Services' and tap 'Roaming'.
   c. Toggle 'Activate International Roaming' to ON.
   d. Confirm acceptance of standard billing terms and select your departure date.
2. USSD Shortcode (Sri Lanka):
   a. Dial #107# from your handset keypad.
   b. Select option 'Activate Roaming' and enter your NIC number or Passport number for verification.
   c. You will receive an SMS confirmation within 15 minutes stating "International Roaming successfully provisioned."
3. Handset Configuration upon Landing:
   a. Access smartphone Settings -> Mobile Data.
   b. Toggle 'Data Roaming' to 'On'.
   c. Leave Network Selection on 'Automatic'.
